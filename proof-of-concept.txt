Why this notebook exists

The aim is to test a narrow claim in the smallest possible setting:

Does a two-channel representation with 2×2 mixing learn differences/oppositions more cleanly than a scalar-weight network, under the same data and training loop?

This phase deliberately avoids attention, large corpora, and heavy hardware. It’s meant to be fast, falsifiable, and easy to extend.

What to compare

The notebook reports:

match accuracy (antonym vs non-antonym)

polarity accuracy (on true antonyms)

(recommended next) swap-consistency metrics: does swapping inputs preserve match while flipping polarity?
